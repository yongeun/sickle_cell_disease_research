## Statement of the problem

Cardiovascular–Kidney–Metabolic (CKM) syndrome now organizes obesity, type 2 diabetes, chronic kidney disease, and cardiovascular disease into a single staged continuum (AHA stages 0–4). The 2026 multi-society CKM guideline reframes the clinical goal from treating end-stage events to **preventing stage progression and promoting stage regression** — a goal that lands first in the primary-care exam room, where family physicians manage the early, still-reversible stages.

GLP-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1RAs) are uniquely matched to this continuum, acting simultaneously on weight, glycemia, blood pressure, and kidney and cardiovascular risk. Yet the pivotal trials (SELECT, FLOW, SUMMIT) enrolled patients already at **CKM stages 3–4** and measured **hard events**, not stage movement. Whether starting a GLP-1RA earlier — at **CKM stage 1 or 2** — keeps patients from advancing, or moves them back down the ladder, in a **real-world, diverse U.S. population** is unknown. That is the question this project answers.

## Central hypothesis

Among adults with early-stage CKM syndrome (stages 1–2), **initiation of a GLP-1RA is associated with a lower rate of CKM-stage progression and a higher rate of stage regression** over follow-up, compared with clinically similar patients who do not initiate a GLP-1RA — with benefits and uptake differing across sociodemographic groups.

## Specific aims

- **Aim 0 (feasibility/power).** Using the *All of Us* Registered/Controlled Tier, operationalize CKM stages 0–4 from EHR diagnoses, labs, and physical measurements (leveraging published *All of Us* CKM-staging and ICD-10-CM frameworks) and enumerate GLP-1RA new-initiators by baseline stage to confirm analyzable sample size, especially at stages 1–2.
- **Aim 1 (primary).** In an **active-comparator, new-user target-trial emulation**, estimate the association between GLP-1RA initiation and **CKM-stage transition** (progression and regression) among stage 1–2 adults, using propensity-based adjustment and negative-control outcomes.
- **Aim 2 (equity).** Test heterogeneity of both **GLP-1RA uptake** and **estimated effect** across race/ethnicity, sex, income, insurance, and rurality, quantifying who is reached and who benefits.

## Approach (brief)

- **Data:** *All of Us* Researcher Workbench — EHR, medications, labs, physical measurements, and survey/social-determinant data on 400,000+ participants with EHR linkage; a resource explicitly designed for population diversity.
- **Design:** Target-trial emulation with a new-user, active-comparator anchor; a defined grace period and landmarking to avoid immortal-time bias; propensity-score overlap weighting; pre-specified sensitivity analyses for adherence (fills/refills), unmeasured confounding (E-values, negative controls), and follow-up truncation.
- **Outcome:** Time to first CKM-stage change (progression or regression), with stage re-assessed at repeated encounters.
- **Rigor & transparency:** Analysis plan pre-registered; code shared; results framed as **hypothesis-generating** associational evidence appropriate to observational data.

## Innovation

This is, to our knowledge, the first analysis to treat **CKM-stage transition/regression itself as the outcome** of GLP-1RA therapy, and the first to focus on the **early, preventable stages (1–2)** that trials skipped — the stages family physicians see first. It complements, rather than duplicates, existing *All of Us* CKM-staging (cross-sectional), GLP-1RA event-emulation (late-stage), and sex-heterogeneity work.

## Significance and fit

CKM and GLP-1RAs are at the center of contemporary cardiometabolic medicine, and the REACH Awards target family physicians "uniquely positioned to bridge the gap in CKM care." An early-stage, primary-care–framed, equity-aware answer speaks directly to that audience: a positive signal supports earlier GLP-1RA consideration in prevention; a null or attenuated signal informs stewardship and access priorities. Either result is actionable for front-line CKM care.

(1) A reproducible, shareable CKM-staging + GLP-1RA cohort pipeline in *All of Us*; (2) the first real-world estimates of GLP-1RA association with **early CKM-stage progression/regression**; (3) an equity map of GLP-1RA reach and benefit — collectively a pilot foundation for a larger extramural (e.g., AHA/NIH) proposal.
